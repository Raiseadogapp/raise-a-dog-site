About This App

I Want to Raise a Dog helps you learn what caring for a dog takes: a 6-month challenge with a digital dog, a journal for a real dog, and Jo’s Community.

The care notes are general information, not veterinary advice. For your own dog’s health, ask a veterinarian. In an emergency, call your vet or an emergency animal clinic right away.

Stay Safe

Walks happen on real streets. Watch where you’re going, stop before you look at your phone, and follow traffic rules. Never use the app while driving or cycling.

The bath workout and walks are exercise. Go at your own pace, skip any move that doesn’t suit your body, and talk to a doctor first if you have a health condition. You exercise at your own risk.

You must be at least 13 years old. If you’re under the age of majority where you live, please use the app with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Your Account and Location

Location is used for walks, home and Say hi on walks, only when you allow it. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone’s settings. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted.

Jo’s Community
