Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

The Short Version

We collect only what the app needs to work. No ads, no tracking, no selling or renting your information. Your walk routes and your real dog’s diary stay on your phone unless you choose to share. You can delete your account and everything online that’s linked to it, in the app, at any time.

What We Collect, and Why

  • Your account (if you sign in): your email address, to sign in with a code; a password if you set one (stored scrambled by our sign-in service, never readable by us).
  • Your profile: first and last name, which are private (only you can read them back; they go on your certificate), and a nickname, which other members see.
  • Your digital dog: the game is kept on your phone. If you share it with helpers, a copy goes online for them, without your home point or walk routes.
  • Your home point (only if you share your digital dog with helpers): kept online so the app can answer “is the helper at the dog’s home?” with a yes or no. Nobody can read the point back, and it can’t move again for 3 days after it’s set.
  • Location on walks: used on your phone to draw the walk map and count a walk. Routes stay on your phone. While a helper holds the leash, their path for that walk is shared with you, the owner. To pass the leash, positions of the dog’s team are kept for 2 minutes, only to check you’re within about 50 m of each other.
  • Say hi on walks (off unless you switch it on): while you walk, a rough area (about 150 m) is kept for 3 minutes so nearby members see “a dog is passing by” with your dog’s name, breed and photo, never a map, a spot or your name.
  • Your real dog’s diary: on your phone. If you share a dog with family or helpers, its profile and diary are online, visible only to that dog’s members.
  • The community: your posts, photos, replies, thumbs, poll votes, reports and blocks, and your dog’s card (name, breed, photo). Members see posts and replies; vote totals are shown, never who voted.
  • Help Other Dogs (off unless you switch it on): monthly totals (breed, age band, walk minutes, spending), shown to others only as averages of 20 dogs or more.
  • Walk cards are saved to your Photos only when you tap Save to Photos.

Who Handles It

  • Supabase stores accounts, the community and shared dogs (our database, sign-in and photo storage).
  • Our email provider sends sign-in codes.
  • OpenFreeMap serves map pieces for the walk map; like any website, it sees your internet address and which map area is requested.
  • Apple and Google handle purchases under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

They may only use your information to provide their service to us. We don’t use analytics or advertising services.

How Long We Keep It

  • Account, profile, posts, replies and votes: until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account removes all of them at once.
  • Shared dogs: a dog you own, with its shared diary, is deleted with your account. Diary entries you added to someone else’s dog stay with that dog, without your name, because they’re part of that dog’s care record.
  • Positions for passing the leash: 2 minutes (removed within 10). Say hi on walks: 3 minutes. A helper’s path: until the dog’s next walk.
  • Reports: kept while Jo’s team reviews them, and with the record of what was decided.

Your Choices

  • Location: allow or stop it in the app or in your phone’s Settings. Without it, walks still count by steps.
  • Delete a post, a photo, a dog or your account in the app. Deleting your account removes your account and everything online linked to it, except diary entries you added to someone else’s dog (kept without your name). Diaries on your phone stay unless you delete the app.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it: email iwantoraiseadog@gmail.com.

Where It’s Stored

Online information is stored by Supabase, our service provider, in the United States (Amazon Web Services, East US, Northern Virginia). Supabase may only use it to run the app for us, under its data processing agreement, and it is protected by encryption and access controls.

Because it is stored in the United States, it may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities there under US law. Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider, which may also process your email address outside your province or country.

Do Not Track

The app doesn’t track you across other apps or websites and has no advertising, so “Do Not Track” settings don’t change anything here.

Children

The app is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Your Rights in Canada

We follow Canada’s private-sector privacy law (PIPEDA) and, for people in Quebec, Quebec’s privacy law. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to our privacy contact, Grant, the app’s owner, at iwantoraiseadog@gmail.com. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec the Commission d’accès à l’information.

Your Rights in the United States

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can delete your account in the app. We don’t sell or share your information for advertising.

Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Questions: iwantoraiseadog@gmail.com.
